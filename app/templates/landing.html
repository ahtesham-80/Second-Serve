Streamlined Flow

How Second Serve Works

From kitchen surplus to community meal distribution in 4 transparent, verified, and automated steps.

1

Donor Lists Surplus

Kitchens specify food category, portions, packaging, hygiene photo, and strict collection deadline in under 60 seconds.

2

Smart Matching

Deterministic algorithm calculates suitability across Category (40%), Location (25%), Quantity (20%), and Urgency (15%).

3

1-Click Atomic Lock

Verified NGO reserves the food with zero duplicate claims. The donation locks instantly to prevent wasted volunteer trips.

4

Collection Stepper

Both parties coordinate through a real-time 4-step collection pipeline: Submitted → Accepted → Ready → Collected.

Deterministic Intelligence

The Math Behind Second Serve

Second Serve eliminates guesswork with an explainable, 100% deterministic rule-based formula. Every match provides an audible score breakdown.

1. Category Compatibility 40% Weight

Exact category matches score 100 pts; compatible cooked food types score 60-80 pts.

2. Geographic Proximity & Locality 25% Weight

Same locality: 100 pts. Same city: 70 pts. Same district: 40 pts. Prioritizes nearest teams.

3. Quantity Capacity Fit 20% Weight

Evaluates batch size against NGO volunteer capacity so food is completely cleared.

4. Deadline Urgency 15% Weight

Imminent cutoffs (< 2 hours) jump to the top of NGO discovery radars for emergency dispatch.
